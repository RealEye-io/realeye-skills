The benchmark data in `data/` and `assets/benchmark/` and the summary in `references/benchmark-summary.md` are **not** covered by that licence:

- **Screenshots** in `assets/benchmark/` show the public first screen of third-party websites as captured on 2026-11-13 and 2026-11-16. The websites, their content, logos and trademarks belong to their owners. They are included only so the analysis can show what each measured page looked like.
- **Attention measurements and heatmaps** are predictions made by the RealEye Attention Predictor, not measured eye tracking, clicks or conversion data. They describe one capture of each page; the pages may have changed since.
- **Group labels** ("top", "average", "poorly made") describe how the pages were selected for the benchmark (see `references/benchmark-summary.md`). The "poorly made" label is one reviewer's judgement of a single first-screen capture against written criteria, not a statement about the companies, their products or their services.

You may use the benchmark data to analyse and compare landing pages with this skill. Do not redistribute the screenshots on their own or present the data as measured user behaviour.

Website owners who want their page removed from the benchmark can open an issue in the repository.

Scoring new pages requires an account and API token for the RealEye Attention Predictor (https://app.attentionpredictor.com).
